# R22 furnished pavilion

Four technical renders come from one saved spatial scene. Three AI material studies use those camera renders and one common appearance reference. The studies preserve the broad layout on visual inspection, but are NOT exact multi-view photographic captures; furniture contours, vases, reflections, glass-leaf details, foliage and fine surface detail can differ. Do not use them as a validated multi-image reconstruction pack. The GLB is the spatial input. Marble import and generation remain untested.

New scope: oak and KARATS AUTO centered on the gate axis, lounge sofa/rug/stone table, office desk with two client chairs and one staff chair, cabinetry, real ceiling light slots and HVAC details. Furniture does not intersect five display envelopes or central aisle. Sightlines from gate to actual brand/oak surfaces checked before and after GLB export. Door beside former oakwall removed. No claims of building-code, structural or full turning-path compliance.
